A&E RapidBrief: Emergency Medical Record Synthesis

Global Benchmark & Problem Introduction

With rapid AI integration globally, tech-forward hubs are pioneering advanced applications to optimise operational workflows. In China, hospitals are using AI tools like Ant A-Fu at the China-Japan Friendship Hospital to organise patient histories and highlight urgent warning signs before a human clinician begins evaluation.

This presents a prime opportunity to elevate Singapore’s emergency infrastructure. Field insights with healthcare professionals reveal the immense stress levels that A&E staff experience under severe time scarcity during critical trauma admissions. Although Singapore’s National Electronic Health Record (NEHR) centralises historical data, manually parsing raw, scattered records under intense pressure leaves dangerous clinical blind spots such as missed drug allergies or active anticoagulants.

The Product Hypothesis

If we deploy a zero-click, automated clinical record synthesis tool built natively on top of Singapore’s national agentic AI infrastructure (Synapxe AgentSea / Tandem), we consolidate scattered longitudinal patient histories into an instant risk briefing, significantly reducing cognitive load during acute A&E intake.

Trial Validation Setup: Parallel Trauma Bay Comparison

To rigorously validate impact, we deploy RapidBrief in 1 active trauma bay (Pilot Bay) while tracking an adjacent control trauma bay (Control Bay) handling identical patient intake volume over a 4-week trial period. We compare clinical assessment speed, risk omission rates, and clinician cognitive strain.

Risk Matrix & Validation Methods

Risk 1: AI Hallucinations & Clinical Accuracy

AI misinterprets complex medical history or fabricates clinical data, risking patient safety.

Validation Strategy: Offline historical RAG accuracy audits comparing AI summaries against human clinician reviews + mandatory direct source provenance linking for every insight.

Risk 2: Legacy EHR Schema Incompatibility & Latency

Incompatible legacy EHR formats cause data pipeline bottlenecks or slow data aggregation, delaying urgent intake.

Validation Strategy: Executing sandbox API integration stress testing under simulated load to enforce a strict <2-second synthesis benchmark.

Risk 3: Clinician Adoption & Alert Fatigue

High-pressure clinicians ignore alerts if the UI introduces clutter or false positives.

Validation Strategy: Conducting post-shift micro-surveys and tracking engagement analytics in parallel trauma bay trials to evaluate user trust and friction.

Future Scalability & Ecosystem Roadmap

High-Acuity Healthcare Expansion

Whole-of-Government (WoG) Integration

Expand consent-gated data pipelines across SPF (traffic incident logs), MOM (WICA workplace injury claims), and AfterLifeSG (automated administrative pre-filing during bereavement).

Notion 3.0 Ideathon: Date our Dates

2nd Runner-Up | AI & System Architecture Case Study

Context: "Goldfish Memory" in Relationships

Couples often struggle to recall past dining experiences, activity preferences, and spontaneous gift ideas. Notes taken during dates are highly unstructured and scattered across different messaging apps, leading to forgotten milestones and arguments over "I told you before."

The Pain Point: External Information Overload

When attempting to plan future dates, couples are bombarded by social media feeds filled with generic, heavily hyped, or paid sponsorships. These recommendations lack relevance and fail to suit the couple's shared, proven tastes.

The Gap

The core issue is the absence of a centralised, personalised system that both remembers past shared enjoyment and uses that data to autonomously inform future, high-quality, local recommendations.

LLM & Notion MCP Integration

We architected a solution that leverages LLMs connected directly to a user's Notion workspace via the Model Context Protocol (MCP). This transforms raw text input into a structured, searchable relational database.

Data Ingestion

Users input unstructured thoughts:
"Movie was okay, that new Thai place we tried, Tom Yum Palace, was amazing (9/10)! So expensive though. He mentioned wanting to try rock climbing next."

create-database-row

Idea Querying

Users can query their workspace naturally:
"What should I get for her birthday next month?"

search-notion

Result: "Note from Feb 12: She mentioned wanting a specific local ceramic mug."

Real-Time Food Discovery Database

To ensure recommendations are up-to-date with local trends, the system utilizes a Note API connector to automatically pull and parse JSON data from local food blog RSS feeds (e.g., Eatbook.sg) directly into a Notion RSS Feed database.
