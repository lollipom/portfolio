A&E RapidBrief: Emergency Medical Record Synthesis

Global Benchmark & Problem Introduction

With rapid AI integration globally, tech-forward hubs are pioneering advanced applications to optimise operational workflows. In China, hospitals are using AI tools like Ant A-Fu at the China-Japan Friendship Hospital to organise patient histories and highlight urgent warning signs before a human clinician begins evaluation.

This presents a prime opportunity to elevate Singapore’s emergency infrastructure. Field insights with healthcare professionals reveal the immense stress levels that A&E staff experience under severe time scarcity during critical trauma admissions. Although Singapore’s National Electronic Health Record (NEHR) centralises historical data, manually parsing raw, scattered records under intense pressure leaves dangerous clinical blind spots such as missed drug allergies or active anticoagulants.

The Product Hypothesis

If we deploy a zero-click, automated clinical record synthesis tool built natively on top of Singapore’s national agentic AI infrastructure (Synapxe AgentSea / Tandem), we consolidate scattered longitudinal patient histories into an instant risk briefing, significantly reducing cognitive load during acute A&E intake.

Trial Validation Setup: Parallel Trauma Bay Comparison

To rigorously validate impact, we deploy RapidBrief in 1 active trauma bay (Pilot Bay) while tracking an adjacent control trauma bay (Control Bay) handling identical patient intake volume over a 4-week trial period. We compare clinical assessment speed, risk omission rates, and clinician cognitive strain.

Risk Matrix & Validation Methods

Risk 1: AI Hallucinations & Clinical Accuracy

AI misinterprets complex medical history or fabricates clinical data, risking patient safety.

Validation Strategy: Offline historical RAG accuracy audits comparing AI summaries against human clinician reviews + mandatory direct source provenance linking for every insight.

Risk 2: Legacy EHR Schema Incompatibility & Latency

Incompatible legacy EHR formats cause data pipeline bottlenecks or slow data aggregation, delaying urgent intake.

Validation Strategy: Executing sandbox API integration stress testing under simulated load to enforce a strict <2-second synthesis benchmark.

Risk 3: Clinician Adoption & Alert Fatigue

High-pressure clinicians ignore alerts if the UI introduces clutter or false positives.

Validation Strategy: Conducting post-shift micro-surveys and tracking engagement analytics in parallel trauma bay trials to evaluate user trust and friction.

Notion 3.0 Ideathon: Date our Dates

2nd Runner-Up | AI & System Architecture Case Study

Context: "Goldfish Memory" in Relationships

Couples often struggle to recall past dining experiences, activity preferences, and spontaneous gift ideas. Notes taken during dates are highly unstructured and scattered across different messaging apps, leading to forgotten milestones and arguments over "I told you before."

The Pain Point: External Information Overload

When attempting to plan future dates, couples are bombarded by social media feeds filled with generic, heavily hyped, or paid sponsorships. These recommendations lack relevance and fail to suit the couple's shared, proven tastes.

The Gap

The core issue is the absence of a centralised, personalised system that both remembers past shared enjoyment and uses that data to autonomously inform future, high-quality, local recommendations.

LLM & Notion MCP Integration

We architected a solution that leverages LLMs connected directly to a user's Notion workspace via the Model Context Protocol (MCP). This transforms raw text input into a structured, searchable relational database.

Zalora: Reimagining the User Experience

The Cost of Clutter

In e-commerce, a cluttered interface is a direct driver of customer attrition. Research indicates that 88% of users won't return after a bad user experience. For Zalora, an overwhelming UI was actively creating confusion, masking product value, and driving shoppers away.

Competitive Deficit

Market analysis revealed Zalora lagging in user preference at 25%, steadily losing ground to cleaner, more intuitive regional competitors like Shopee (40%) and Lazada (35%).

User Research Validation

Conducted targeted user surveys with 15 respondents. A staggering 13 out of 15 users identified a "cluttered and inconsistent UI" as their primary pain point, resulting in severe drop-offs before checkout.

The Product Vision

A Zalora experience that does not only sell fashion, but feels like fashion—elevated, aesthetic, and effortlessly seamless. We needed to transition from frustration to a fashion-first discovery engine.

Streamlining the Core Journey

My primary focus during the prototyping phase was to aggressively declutter the interface and rebuild the information architecture. By focusing on the Homepage, Tab Bar, and Checkout Process, I architected a seamless flow designed to reduce cognitive load and prioritize product visibility.

1

Homepage Overhaul

Simplified the previously overwhelming homepage into 3 key, digestible sections.

  • Introduced a curated Lookbook section (e.g., highlighting "cottagecore" and trending aesthetics) to provide users with cohesive outfit inspiration rather than isolated, discounted items.
  • Replaced dense promotional banners with targeted, style-driven discovery zones.
2

Tab Bar Re-Architecture

Redesigned the primary navigation to prioritize standard e-commerce mental models and high-frequency actions.

  • Moved the Wishlist icon to the top right of the application header, making it universally accessible from any screen without consuming valuable tab bar space.
  • Streamlined the bottom tab bar to five distinct, focused destinations: Home, Categories, Messages, Bag, Profile.
3

Frictionless Checkout Process

Aggressively cut visual noise in the shopping cart to keep the user entirely focused on their purchasing intent.

  • Grouped item descriptions with consistent typography and implemented highly prominent product checkboxes.
  • Deferred clutter: Removed distracting information such as full shipping addresses and detailed order summaries from the initial cart review step, shifting them strictly to the final payment confirmation page.
  • Redesigned the VIP subscription prompt to clearly and concisely present benefits without obstructing the checkout button.

Validation & User Testing

Conducted low-fidelity prototype walkthroughs with our original 15 user testing participants to validate the redesign's flow, layout clarity, and brand perception.

GovTech x NTU Product Club Hackathon: Scholarship Workflow

THE CHALLENGE: FRAGMENTED LEGACY WORKFLOWS

Managing and evaluating scholarship applications in Singapore’s higher education ecosystem involves highly fragmented systems, manual document cross-referencing, and communication bottlenecks. Students are forced to repeatedly enter static demographic and transcript data across separate university and government agency portals, while review committees spend hundreds of hours manually verifying certificates and computing eligibility scores.

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS

For Applicants (Students)

  • Redundant re-entry of personal, household income, and academic data.
  • Unclear, black-box processing stages leaving applicants uncertain for months.
  • Inconsistent document submission requirements causing last-minute rejections.

For Reviewers & Sponsors

  • Manual authenticity checks on academic transcripts and referee letters.
  • Lack of standardized candidate evaluation rubrics across departments.
  • High cognitive overhead when comparing multi-criteria applications under tight deadlines.

OUR STRATEGIC PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS

By establishing a unified, Singpass-integrated digital scholarship workflow platform, we can eliminate 80% of manual data entry, automate eligibility scoring via pre-verified databases, and deliver a frictionless journey for applicants while granting reviewers real-time analytics and decision-support tools.

Core Features

Base product for onboarding agencies

  • Build customized forms according to scholarship requirements
  • Supports retrieval of basic information from SingPass
  • Routing function to other departments within agency
  • Publish on their website & Data export capabilities

Additional Enhancements

ATS System (Agency POV)

Enhancing Base Product:

  • Automatically screen applications based on predefined criteria
  • Enables bulk processing, tagging, and filtering across different stages
  • Supports collaborative review by multiple evaluators with role-based access
  • Manages large volumes of applications more efficiently

3-Phase Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

Designed for seamless institutional adoption, moving from university MVP pilots to full national cross-agency consolidation.

1

Phase 1: NTU Pilot & University MVP Validation

Months 1 – 4

Deploy MVP in collaboration with NTU Financial Aid & Scholarship Office focusing on high-volume undergraduate merit scholarships.

  • Validate applicant submission completion rates and drop-off reductions.
  • Gather UX feedback from university selection panel members to refine rubric customizability.
2

Phase 2: Multi-Autonomous University Scaling

Months 5 – 10

Expand integration across NUS, SMU, SUTD, SIT, and SUSS, establishing a unified database schema for higher education scholarships in Singapore.

  • Standardize OpenCerts API integrations for instant transcript verification across all local polytechnics and A-Level portals.
  • Enable multi-university application matching to prevent duplicate award acceptances.
3

Phase 3: National Cross-Agency & GovTech Integration

Months 11 – 18

Consolidate statutory board and ministry scholarship sponsors (e.g., PSC, A*STAR, EDB) into a single Whole-of-Government (WoG) portal.

  • Full Singpass integration for automated financial background assessment.
  • Centralized cross-agency candidate dashboard enabling joint interview scheduling.

Risk Matrix & Compliance

Risk 1: Data Privacy & Singpass Integration Compliance

Handling sensitive student financial and household data requires strict adherence to PDPA and Government Data Security Standards.

Mitigation: Enforce role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end encryption for stored records, and explicit consent toggles for cross-agency data sharing.

Risk 2: Legacy Database System Resistance

Universities rely on legacy ERP platforms (e.g., Peoplesoft, SAP) with custom database schemas.

Mitigation: Build standardized RESTful API adapters and staging tables to enable dual-directional synchronization without overriding core university infrastructure.
