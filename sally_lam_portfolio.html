A&E RapidBrief: Emergency Medical Record Synthesis

Global Benchmark & Problem Introduction

With rapid AI integration globally, tech-forward hubs are pioneering advanced applications to optimise operational workflows. In China, hospitals are using AI tools like Ant A-Fu at the China-Japan Friendship Hospital to organise patient histories and highlight urgent warning signs before a human clinician begins evaluation.

This presents a prime opportunity to elevate Singapore’s emergency infrastructure. Field insights with healthcare professionals reveal the immense stress levels that A&E staff experience under severe time scarcity during critical trauma admissions. Although Singapore’s National Electronic Health Record (NEHR) centralises historical data, manually parsing raw, scattered records under intense pressure leaves dangerous clinical blind spots such as missed drug allergies or active anticoagulants.

The Product Hypothesis

If we deploy a zero-click, automated clinical record synthesis tool built natively on top of Singapore’s national agentic AI infrastructure (Synapxe AgentSea / Tandem), we consolidate scattered longitudinal patient histories into an instant risk briefing, significantly reducing cognitive load during acute A&E intake.

GovTech x NTU Product Club Hackathon: Scholarship Workflow

The Workflow Gap: Fragmented Legacy Processing

Managing and evaluating scholarship applications across Singapore’s higher education ecosystem currently suffers from fragmented systems, manual document cross-referencing, and communication bottlenecks. Students are forced to repeatedly enter static demographic and transcript data across separate university and government portals, while review committees spend hundreds of hours manually verifying credentials and computing eligibility scores.

Root Cause Analysis

For Applicants (Students)

  • Redundant re-entry of personal, household income, and academic data across multiple portals.
  • Unclear, black-box processing stages leaving applicants uncertain for months.
  • Inconsistent document submission requirements causing last-minute rejections.

For Reviewers & Sponsors

  • Manual authenticity checks on academic transcripts and referee letters.
  • Lack of standardized candidate evaluation rubrics across departments.
  • High cognitive overhead when comparing multi-criteria applications under tight deadlines.

Our Strategic Product Hypothesis

By establishing a unified, Singpass-integrated digital scholarship workflow platform, we eliminate 80% of manual data entry, automate eligibility scoring via pre-verified databases, and deliver a frictionless journey for applicants while granting reviewers real-time analytics and decision-support tools.

3-Phase Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

Designed for seamless institutional adoption, moving from university MVP pilots to full national cross-agency consolidation.

1

Phase 1: NTU Pilot & University MVP Validation

Months 1 – 4

Deploy MVP in collaboration with NTU Financial Aid & Scholarship Office focusing on high-volume undergraduate merit scholarships.

  • Validate applicant submission completion rates and drop-off reductions.
  • Gather UX feedback from university selection panel members to refine rubric customizability.
2

Phase 2: Multi-Autonomous University Scaling

Months 5 – 10

Expand integration across NUS, SMU, SUTD, SIT, and SUSS, establishing a unified database schema for higher education scholarships in Singapore.

  • Standardize OpenCerts API integrations for instant transcript verification across all local polytechnics and A-Level portals.
  • Enable multi-university application matching to prevent duplicate award acceptances.
3

Phase 3: National Cross-Agency & GovTech Integration

Months 11 – 18

Consolidate statutory board and ministry scholarship sponsors (e.g., PSC, A*STAR, EDB) into a single Whole-of-Government (WoG) portal.

  • Full Singpass integration for automated financial background assessment.
  • Centralized cross-agency candidate dashboard enabling joint interview scheduling.

Risk Matrix & Compliance

Risk 1: Data Privacy & Singpass Integration Compliance

Handling sensitive student financial and household data requires strict adherence to PDPA and Government Data Security Standards.

Mitigation: Enforce role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end encryption for stored records, and explicit consent toggles for cross-agency data sharing.
Risk 2: Legacy Database System Resistance

Universities rely on legacy ERP platforms (e.g., Peoplesoft, SAP) with custom database schemas.

Mitigation: Build standardized RESTful API adapters and staging tables to enable dual-directional synchronization without overriding core university infrastructure.
